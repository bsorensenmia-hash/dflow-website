## Phase 3: Market Research (Days 8-10)

### 3.1 Identify High-Probability Clients
Target businesses with:
- Annual revenue $500K-$5M (can afford fees, need grants)
- Growth stage (hiring, expanding)
- Innovation focus (R&D, tech adoption)
- Social impact mission (easier to sell story)

### 3.2 Competitive Analysis
Research local grant writers:
- What do they charge? ($1,500-$5,000/grant typical)
- What's their success rate? (50-70% industry average)
- What industries do they specialize in?
- What's missing in their offerings?

### 3.3 Build Target Grant List
Create spreadsheet with:
- Grant name & agency
- Typical award amount
- Deadline (recurring or one-time)
- Eligibility requirements
- Match/cost-share needed?
- Difficulty level (beginner/intermediate/advanced)
