# Upwork Proposal Template

## Template Structure

```
[CUSTOM OPENING - Reference their specific pain point]

[OUR SOLUTION - 2-3 sentences max]

[RELEVANT CASE STUDY - 1 example matching their need]

[DELIVERABLES - Bullet list]

[TIMELINE - Specific]

[NEXT STEP - Call to action]
```

---

## Variation: Technical Deep-Dive (For Complex Jobs)

**When to use:** Jobs mentioning specific APIs, advanced AI concepts, or technical requirements.

**Additions:**
- Lead with technical understanding: "I see you need [specific API integration]. We've worked extensively with [that API] — here's how we'd approach your [technical challenge]..."
- Include brief technical architecture overview
- Mention specific challenges + solutions (shows you understand complexity)

---

## Variation: Fast Turnaround (For Urgent Jobs)

**When to use:** Jobs with "ASAP", "urgent", "needed quickly" language.

**Additions:**
- Lead with availability: "I can start immediately and deliver within [X days]."
- Emphasize fast delivery in case study
- Offer milestone-based approach for faster initial results

---

## Variation: Enterprise Client (For $10K+ Jobs)

**When to use:** Large budgets, established companies, complex requirements.

**Additions:**
- Lead with business impact (not technical details)
- Use formal tone
- Mention post-launch support, SLAs, documentation
- Reference larger case studies (if available)

---

## DO NOT

❌ Generic "I'm interested in your project" openings
❌ Listing all our skills (they read the profile already)
❌ Promising results we can't guarantee
❌ Copying/pasting same proposal to multiple jobs
❌ Asking questions we could answer by reading their post
❌ Writing novels (keep it under 300 words)

## DO

✅ Reference their specific problem in opening sentence
✅ Lead with solution, not credentials
✅ Include 1 relevant case study (matching their industry/problem)
✅ Clear deliverables + timeline
✅ Call to action (schedule call, answer questions, etc.)
✅ Proofread (zero typos)

---

## CONNECT ALLOCATION

**80 connects/month (free tier) = 16 proposals max (5 connects each)**

Target: 5-8 high-quality proposals/week.

**Only bid on jobs where:**
- Budget matches our range ($3K+ preferred)
- We have relevant case study or capability
- Client has clear problem statement (not vague "looking for AI expert")
- Posted within last 48 hours (higher response rate)

**Avoid:**
- Sub-$1K jobs (time sink)
- "Looking for long-term partner" with no immediate project
- Vague requirements (client doesn't know what they want)
- 20+ proposals already (too much competition)
