## Payment Terms

- **Monthly services:** Invoiced on the 1st, due by the 10th. Auto-pay available via Stripe/QuickBooks.
- **Project work:** 50% upfront, 50% on completion. Projects over $5,000: 40/30/30 milestone split.
- **Late payments:** 5% late fee after 15 days. Services paused after 30 days overdue.
- **Refund policy:** Prorated refund for unused portion of current month. No refunds on completed project milestones.
