# Auction Broker Service — Sorensen Group Corporation

## Concept
Buy surplus/liquidation inventory at auction, resell at markup. Act as broker for clients who want auction deals but don't have time to attend/monitor.

## Target Auction Sources

### Government Surplus
- **GovPlanet.com** — Heavy equipment, vehicles, industrial
- **GSA Auctions (gsaauctions.gov)** — Federal surplus property
- **PublicSurplus.com** — State/local government surplus
- **GovDeals.com** — Municipal vehicles, equipment, electronics

### Liquidation
- **Liquidation.com** — Retail returns, overstock (Amazon, Target, Walmart)
- **B-Stock.com** — Certified liquidation marketplace
- **DirectLiquidation.com** — Electronics, appliances
- **BlueLots.com** — Pallet deals

### Local
- **Storage unit auctions** — StorageTreasures.com
- **Estate auctions** — AuctionZip.com (FL listings)
- **Police auctions** — PropertyRoom.com
- **Broward County surplus** — Check county procurement site

## Service Tiers

### Tier 1: Scout Service ($99/month)
- Weekly email: Top 10 auction opportunities in FL
- Filtered by category preference
- Estimated resale value for each item
- Direct links to bid

### Tier 2: Bid & Buy ($299/month + 15% of purchase)
- Everything in Scout
- We monitor and place bids for you
- Set budget limits, we execute
- Pickup/shipping coordination
- Up to 5 purchases/month

### Tier 3: Full Broker (20% of profit)
- Everything in Bid & Buy
- We handle resale (eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist)
- Profit split: 80% client / 20% Sorensen
- Monthly accounting and reporting
- Unlimited purchases
