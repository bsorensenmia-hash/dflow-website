## Sequence Notes

**Pre-Send Research (Per Lead):**
- [ ] Run their site through Ahrefs/Ubersuggest — get 3 real issues
- [ ] Identify one competitor outranking them for a valuable keyword
- [ ] Estimate their customer value for ROI email
- [ ] Check their Google Business Profile for local SEO angle

**Personalization is Non-Negotiable:**
- Email 1 MUST have 3 real issues from their actual site
- Email 2 MUST have a real competitor and real keyword
- Generic versions of these emails will not convert

**Sending Best Practices:**
- Send Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM in the prospect's timezone
- Max 30 emails/day on a new domain, scale over 2 weeks
- Track opens — manual follow-up on 2+ opens without reply
- Remove bounces same day

**Tools:**
- Research: Ahrefs, Ubersuggest, Google Search Console (via audit)
- Sourcing: Google Maps (page 2+ results = they need SEO), Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Sending: Instantly.ai or Smartlead
- Warmup: Instantly warmup
