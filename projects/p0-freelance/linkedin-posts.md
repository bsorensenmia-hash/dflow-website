## Post 3: The Value-First How-To

---

**5 things I automate for every new client (and you can too):**

1️⃣ **Invoice follow-ups** — QuickBooks auto-sends a reminder at 7, 14, and 30 days overdue. Stop chasing payments manually.

2️⃣ **New lead → CRM** — Form submission triggers a Zapier workflow: contact goes into your CRM, you get a Slack notification, and the lead gets a welcome email. Takes 15 minutes to set up.

3️⃣ **Weekly financial snapshot** — Every Monday at 8 AM, you get a P&L summary and cash position for the week. Automated from QuickBooks + Google Sheets.

4️⃣ **Receipt capture** — Clients photograph receipts → they go into a shared Google Drive folder → I categorize them monthly. No more shoeboxes.

5️⃣ **Sales tax calculation** — For FL businesses: tax rates auto-applied based on county. No more Googling "what's the sales tax in Hillsborough County."

The ROI on these 5 automations? About 8-12 hours/month saved for the average small business owner.

**Time is money. Stop spending it on things a robot can do.**

## General LinkedIn Strategy Notes

**Posting Cadence:** 3x/week minimum (Mon, Wed, Fri or Tue, Thu, Sat)

**Content Mix:**
- 40% Case studies / results (Post 1 style)
- 30% Contrarian takes / industry commentary (Post 2 style)
- 30% Value-first how-tos / tips (Post 3 style)

**Profile Optimization:**
- Headline: "I help small businesses get clean books, better SEO, and automated workflows | Founder, Sorensen Group Corporation"
- Banner: Service offerings + scheduling link
- Featured: Case study PDF, free audit offer, scheduling link

**Engagement Rules:**
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours of posting
- Comment on 10 posts in your niche daily (before and after your post)
- DM everyone who engages meaningfully — no pitch, just conversation first
- Never use LinkedIn's default connection message
