## Proposal Body

Hi {{client_name}},

{{specific_detail_from_posting}} — I've written successful proposals for similar programs and I'm confident I can deliver a compelling application for you.

**My approach to grant writing:**

1. **Funder Research & Alignment** — Before I write a word, I verify that your organization and project are a strong match for the funder's priorities. No point writing a beautiful proposal for the wrong grant.
2. **Needs Statement & Logic Model** — I build the narrative around data-backed need and a clear theory of change. Reviewers score higher when they can follow the logic.
3. **Program Design Support** — If the program design needs tightening, I'll flag it. A great narrative can't save a weak program.
4. **Budget & Budget Narrative** — Realistic, justified, and formatted to the funder's specifications.
5. **Review & Polish** — Every proposal gets at least two rounds of self-editing before delivery. Clean, compliant, compelling.

**What sets me apart:**

- I ask the hard questions upfront so we don't waste cycles on a proposal that won't score.
- I write in clear, direct language — not grant-speak. Reviewers read 50+ proposals per cycle. Yours will be the one they remember.
- I deliver drafts early so you have time to review and iterate.

**Relevant experience:** {{e.g., "Wrote a successful $150K federal grant for a workforce development nonprofit" or "Completed 12 grant applications in the past year across federal, state, and foundation funders."}}.

I'm ready to review the RFP/guidelines and give you an honest assessment of fit and timeline.

Best,
**Andrew Sorensen**
Sorensen Group Corporation

## Customization Notes
- Always ask for the full RFP or funding announcement before quoting
- For federal grants (grants.gov): mention familiarity with SF-424, logic models, and compliance requirements
- For foundation grants: emphasize narrative storytelling and relationship-building language
- For nonprofits: reference 501(c)(3) documentation, organizational capacity sections
- Specify turnaround time based on grant complexity — don't promise "fast" for a 50-page federal app
