## Customization Notes
- Always quantify the time savings in the proposal when possible
- For Zapier/Make jobs: mention specific "zaps" or "scenarios" you'd build
- For CRM automation: ask which CRM they use and mention specific integrations
- For AI automation jobs: lead with AI capabilities and mention specific use cases
- Frame everything as ROI — hours saved, errors eliminated, revenue recovered
