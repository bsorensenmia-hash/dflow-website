## Lead Qualification System

### Tier 1: Hot Lead ($50)
- Requested quote within 24 hours
- Provided phone + email
- Specific insurance type selected
- In target geography

### Tier 2: Warm Lead ($25)
- Downloaded guide or submitted form
- Provided email only
- General interest
- May need nurturing

### Tier 3: Cold Lead ($15)
- Clicked ad but didn't complete form
- Retargeting eligible
- Email list subscriber
