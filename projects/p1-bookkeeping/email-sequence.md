## Sequence Notes

**Personalization Checklist:**
- [ ] First name from business registration or LinkedIn
- [ ] Business type (restaurant, contractor, medical, etc.)
- [ ] Florida city for localization
- [ ] Similar business type for social proof emails

**Sending Best Practices:**
- Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM EST
- Use a warm domain (send 20-30/day max on a new domain, scale up over 2 weeks)
- Track opens and clicks — follow up manually on opens without replies
- Remove bounces immediately to protect sender reputation

**Tools:**
- Sourcing: Florida SunBiz, Google Maps, Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Sending: Instantly.ai, Smartlead, or Woodpecker
- Warmup: Instantly warmup or Mailreach
