# Florida Small Business Grants Database 2025–2026

*Compiled by Mycroft Intelligence | Sorensen Group Corporation*
*Last Updated: April 1, 2026*

## Overview

Florida offers approximately $31.9M in state grants and $22.7M in local government funding for small businesses (<500 employees). The state also has $488M available through SSBCI 2.0 (federal pass-through). Below are 15 verified grant programs currently open or cyclically available.

---

## STATE-LEVEL GRANTS

### 1. Enterprise Florida Trade Grants
- **Award:** Varies by program (reimbursement-based, up to $5,000–$15,000 per grant)
- **Deadline:** Rolling / multiple cycles per year (max 3 grants/year per company)
- **Eligibility:** FL-based, 2+ years in business, 3–500 FTE, $250K+ annual revenue, licensed commercial operation (no home-based), manufacturer or approved service provider
- **Programs:** Export Marketing Plans, Gold Key/Matchmaker, Target Sector Trade Shows, Online Global Website Localization
- **URL:** https://selectflorida.org/export-from-florida/trade-grants/
- **Notes:** Great for clients entering international markets. Reimbursement model.

### 2. Florida Small Business Development Center (SBDC) Export Grants
- **Award:** Up to $15,000 reimbursement per funding period
- **Deadline:** Rolling (contact local SBDC office)
- **Eligibility:** FL businesses new to exporting or using exports to grow
- **Covers:** Marketing plans, export credit insurance, trade show expenses, U.S. Commerce Dept. events
- **URL:** https://floridasbdc.org/
- **Notes:** Also provides FREE consulting on business planning, marketing, financial analysis, cybersecurity.

### 3. Florida High Tech Corridor Research Grants
- **Award:** Up to $150,000
- **Deadline:** Rolling applications, reviewed by external committee
- **Eligibility:** Technology companies in 23 designated FL counties; must partner with UCF or USF researchers
- **Use:** R&D collaboration with university partners
- **URL:** https://floridahightech.com/innovation-investments/research-grants/
- **Notes:** Excellent for tech startups with research needs. Requires university collaboration agreement.

### 4. Florida State Small Business Credit Initiative (SSBCI 2.0)
- **Award:** $488M total fund; individual amounts vary by program
- **Deadline:** Ongoing until funds exhausted (additional $167M released Jan 2025)
- **Eligibility:** FL-based, ≤500 employees, focus on very small businesses and socially/economically disadvantaged owners
- **Use:** Equipment, inventory, franchise fees, start-up costs, facility improvements
- **Programs:** 5 sub-programs including loans and venture capital investments
- **URL:** https://www.floridajobs.org/
- **Notes:** Since Jan 2023, FL has deployed $202M+ to 149 businesses, stimulating $769M in private capital.

### 5. Florida Job Growth Grant Fund
- **Award:** Up to $500,000
- **Deadline:** Annual cycles
- **Eligibility:** Local governments and educational institutions (businesses benefit indirectly through workforce training and infrastructure)
- **Use:** Workforce training, public infrastructure development
- **URL:** https://floridajobs.org/jobgrowth
- **Notes:** Benefits businesses through improved workforce and infrastructure. Grant goes to municipality/institution, not directly to business.

### 6. Prospera Small Business Grants (Hispanic Entrepreneurs)
- **Award:** Varies by program and need
- **Deadline:** Rolling / contact Prospera
- **Eligibility:** Hispanic-owned startups and existing small businesses in FL
- **Services Included:** Accounting, branding, business plan creation, legal assessment, marketing/sales plan, QuickBooks consulting
- **URL:** https://prosperausa.org/
- **Notes:** Free strategy session available. Strong for Hispanic-owned businesses.

## LOCAL / REGIONAL GRANTS

### 7. Miami-Dade Mom and Pop Small Business Grant
- **Award:** Varies by commission district (typically $2,500–$10,000)
- **Deadline:** Cyclical by district
- **Eligibility:** Small businesses in individual Miami-Dade commission districts
- **Use:** Inventory, marketing, equipment, minor repairs, insurance, licenses
- **URL:** https://www.miamidade.gov/mom-pop-grants/
- **Notes:** One of the most accessible FL grants. Requires attending a workshop.

### 8. Miami-Dade Economic Advocacy Trust (MDEAT) Business Grant
- **Award:** Up to $7,500 (Small Business Capitalization Program)
- **Deadline:** 2025 grant cycle currently open
- **Eligibility:** Business in existence for minimum 3 years, located in Miami-Dade
- **URL:** https://www.miamidade.gov/economicadvocacytrust/
- **Notes:** Second grant cycle announced. Two distinct opportunity tracks.

### 9. Orlando Business Assistance Program
- **Award:** Up to $20,000 (50% matching grant)
- **Deadline:** Rolling / contact City of Orlando Economic Development
- **Eligibility:** Small businesses physically located within Orlando city limits
- **Use:** Transportation impact fees, permits, sidewalk repairs, street improvements, water line construction, landscaping, EV charging
- **URL:** https://www.orlando.gov/Building-Development/Business-Incentives/Business-Incentive-Programs/Business-Assistance-Program
- **Notes:** Dollar-for-dollar match required. Must obtain permits before reimbursement.

### 10. Orlando Gusto Impact Awards
- **Award:** $10,000 grant + $50,000 in free advertising + 1 year free Gusto payroll; 50 runners-up get $500 gift cards
- **Deadline:** Annual cycle
- **Eligibility:** 1+ year in operation, ≤75 employees, ≤$7M annual revenue
- **Notes:** Also covers payroll processing. Popular competition format.

### 11. Tampa Downtown Partnership Grant
- **Award:** Varies
- **Deadline:** Cyclical
- **Eligibility:** Businesses in downtown Tampa area
- **Use:** Business development, storefront improvement
- **Notes:** Check with Tampa Downtown Partnership for current cycle.

### 12. City of Miami Small Business Emergency Grant
- **Award:** Varies by cycle
- **Deadline:** Activated during emergencies; check City of Miami site
- **Eligibility:** Small businesses affected by declared emergencies
- **Notes:** Triggered by hurricanes, pandemics, or other emergency declarations.

## PRIVATE / NATIONAL GRANTS AVAILABLE TO FL BUSINESSES

### 13. Nav's $10,000 Quarterly Small Business Grant
- **Award:** $10,000
- **Deadline:** Quarterly cycles
- **Eligibility:** U.S.-based small businesses (FL eligible)
- **URL:** https://www.nav.com/small-business-grant/
- **Notes:** Simple application. Competitive but accessible.

### 14. Visa She's Next Grant (Women Entrepreneurs)
- **Award:** $10,000 + mentorship
- **Deadline:** Annual cycles
- **Eligibility:** Women-owned small businesses in the U.S.
- **Notes:** Requires pitch video and business plan. Strong brand partnership.

### 15. Comcast RISE (Minority Entrepreneurs)
- **Award:** Technology makeover package + marketing support (valued at $10,000+)
- **Deadline:** Rolling monthly applications
- **Eligibility:** Minority-owned small businesses (5+ years in operation preferred)
- **Focus:** Digital media, tech, connectivity
- **URL:** https://www.comcastrise.com/
- **Notes:** Not cash grant — in-kind technology and marketing services.

## FEDERAL PROGRAMS ACCESSIBLE FROM FLORIDA

### SBIR/STTR (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Award:** Phase I: $50,000–$275,000 | Phase II: up to $1M
- **Deadline:** Multiple cycles per year across 11 federal agencies
- **Eligibility:** U.S.-based, for-profit, ≤500 employees, PI must be primarily employed by the business
- **URL:** https://www.sbir.gov/
- **Notes:** $3B+ awarded annually nationwide. Tech/R&D focus. FL has strong representation.
